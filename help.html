FAQs

What is JoMu and how does it work?
JoMu is a business to business platform that connects businesses looking to buy, sell, or collaborate. You can list your products/services, discover potential partners, and start deals directly through the platform.
Who can join the platform?
Any legalised business or verified entrepreneur can join, including manufacturers, wholesalers, retailers, service providers, and startups.
How do I register my business?
Simply click on "Create account", fill in the required information, and submit by clicking "Continue".
Is it free to create an account?
Yes! Creating an account on JoMu is free.
How do I verify another business before making a deal?
You can check their profile, reviews, business documents(if uploaded), or request additional verification before proceeding.
Who is the most trustworthy seller for clothes bale (Ndiboota) on JoMu?
JoMu does not rank or endorse specific sellers, buyers or service providers. However, you can identify trustworthy by checking their reviews, and how complete their profile is. Always connect and communicate clearly before making any deals.
Is there a messaging system to communicate with other businesses?
Yes, our built-in messaging system allows you to chat securely with potential business partners.
Can individuals join the platform?
No, only businesses and verified entrepreneurs.
What to do if I fail to recover my forgotten password?
Send a direct message to JoMu through our social media handles or simply email us for support.
How do I delete my account?
Contact JoMu support team for help.
Can I access the platform without creating an account?
You can browse listings, but you must create an account to connect or post.
How do I report a fake or suspicious business?
Contact JoMu support team with details.
Is my data safe on this platform?
Yes, we use industry-standard practices to protect your data. Read more in the Privacy Policy.
I'm in agriculture. Can I post seasonal products?
Yes, as long as you specify your category(Agriculture), you can list seasonal products and update them based on availability.
Can I post single items like one phone or laptop for retail sale?
No. JoMu is strictly for bulk buying and selling. We do not support single or retail product listings. Please use platforms designed for individual item sales if you're looking to sell or buy single products.
How can I connect with other businesses?
You can browse listings, send direct messages, or request to connect with a business from their profile.
