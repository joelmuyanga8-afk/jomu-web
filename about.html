Hi, Get to know about JoMu.

What is JoMu?

JoMu is a business to business (B2B) wholesale marketplace platform designed to connect businesses and facilitate the buying, selling, and collaboration of products and services. JoMu serves as a hub where businesses can seamlessly engage in trade and partnerships. JoMu was started in 2024.


Vision

Facilitate businesses across Africa through reliable, trustworthy, and digitally enhanced wholesale trade.
